Harbor Refund Policy

Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of purchase.

The request must include the original order reference and the reason for the refund.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Refund processing normally takes between 5 and 10 business days.

Refund Support

Customers who have questions about an existing refund should contact the support team.

The support team may request additional order details before providing a status update.
